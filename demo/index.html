          01 / MEASURE
          
The material sets the fit.

          02 / COMPENSATE
          
Kerf, exactly once.

          

            Full beam width goes in. Closed outlines expand and holes contract. Geometry mode needs
            no extra CAM offset.
          

        

        

          03 / INSPECT
          
Know what was checked.

          

            Dimensions and ideal assembly are testable. The preview does not predict material
